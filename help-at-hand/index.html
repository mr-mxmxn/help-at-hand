🚨 Emergency Guidance

Do not wait for a routine teleconsultation if there is:

  • Unconsciousness
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Severe bleeding
  • Seizure or sudden loss of consciousness
  • Severe chest pain or sudden collapse
  • Any rapidly life-threatening condition

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest health facility immediately.
